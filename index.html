A little clarity, before you visit.
Where is Faraz Pharmacy located?
We are in front of THQ Hospital, Barikot, Swat, Pakistan. Use the Google Maps link above to search for the location.
Can I check if a medicine is available?
Please visit the pharmacy to confirm availability. The website does not show live stock.
How do online order requests work?
Prepare your item list, add your WhatsApp number and optional product photos, review it and submit. Your request is saved privately for pharmacy review. You will receive a reference number. Requests require pharmacy confirmation; payment is not collected on this website.
Should I bring my prescription?
Please bring a valid prescription when requesting prescription medicines.
