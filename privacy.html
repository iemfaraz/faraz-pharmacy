What you submit

When you send a request, we collect your name, WhatsApp number, item list, notes and optional prescription or product photos. For delivery requests, a location pin and address are collected only when you enable location and submit. The Android app does not request background location.

How it is used

The pharmacy uses this information to review the request, communicate with you, prepare quotations and arrange collection or delivery. Requests are enquiries until reviewed and confirmed. Prescription items remain subject to pharmacy verification. No online payment is collected.

Storage and access

The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Private records and uploaded photos are stored in the pharmacy backend using Cloudflare D1 and object storage through its hosting provider. Owner and authorised pharmacy staff use protected dashboard sessions. Inventory-only staff do not have access to customer requests. The pharmacy owner can export private data backups.

Customer shopping accounts

Customer registration stores your name, email, optional phone number, password hash and recovery-code hash in the private pharmacy backend. Your email is a login identifier; email verification and emailed password resets are not enabled. Save your single-use recovery code privately. Customer sessions last up to eight hours and are stored in this browser tab. Customer accounts can access only their own shopping orders, quotations and messages and provide no staff dashboard access. Guest requests remain accessible through their private tracking codes.

Your cart stores product identifiers and quantities in local storage on this device. Cart prices are estimates; the pharmacy confirms the quotation. Logout clears the cart and customer session on this page. Contact the pharmacy for account-data requests.

Your tracking link

Your reference and private code allow access to request status, quotation and messages. Keep them private. A quotation response is saved with your request. The pharmacy must confirm supply and delivery.

Other services

WhatsApp, phone, email and map links open the services you choose to use. Those services have their own privacy practices. The optional bill reader downloads its OCR/PDF libraries and reads documents in the browser; it does not use a cloud AI provider. Reviewed inventory data is saved only when confirmed.

Retention and requests

Records remain available to the pharmacy for request handling and business records. Contact info@farazpharmacy.com to ask about access, correction or deletion, or to report a privacy concern. Requests are reviewed by the pharmacy; deletion may depend on applicable recordkeeping requirements. This website does not currently provide automated deletion or a fixed automatic retention period.

Use only necessary information

Avoid identity documents, unrelated medical records and personal medical history. Do not use this service for emergencies. Contact the pharmacy directly if you need help understanding a request.

Delivery and hours

Published hours, delivery areas, charges and times appear on the home page once confirmed by the pharmacy. If a detail is not published, contact the pharmacy before ordering. A delivery-location pin does not guarantee availability or delivery timing.
